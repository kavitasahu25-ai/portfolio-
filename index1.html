Thoughts

Creative Learning & Visual Clarity

Creative learning begins when an idea feels approachable rather than intimidating. Whether filming a lively campus moment or arranging visuals on a page, visual clarity helps people notice details, connect patterns, and stay curious.

Hands-on making teaches things that reading alone often misses—how light falls, how a sequence flows, and how small adjustments change the whole experience. At CCL, IIT Gandhinagar, I hope to contribute practical visual energy and organized teamwork while learning deeply from making alongside creative minds.
